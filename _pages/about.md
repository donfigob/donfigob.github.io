I am a PhD student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at The Ohio State University and a Graduate Research Assistant at the [Center for Automotive Research](https://car.osu.edu/).

My work focuses on autonomous vehicles, collaborative 3D perception, multi-modal perception, and learning-based decision-making for safety-critical autonomy. I am advised by Prof. [Qadeer Ahmed](https://mae.osu.edu/people/ahmed.358) and Dr. [Ekim Yurtsever](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=dJGmJCEAAAAJ&hl=en).

As of August 2026, I am preparing for Summer 2027 internship opportunities in autonomous driving, perception, AV software, and applied machine learning.

## Current Focus

- Collaborative 3D perception for autonomous driving.
- Full-stack autonomous vehicle integration, including perception pipelines, SLAM, control interfaces, and CAN-based vehicle communication.
- Learning-based decision-making for complex driving scenarios.

## Selected Work

- **When the City Teaches the Car:** label-free 3D perception from infrastructure, currently under review for ECCV 2026.
- **SmartCityZero:** label-free 3D perception through RSU-to-vehicle collaboration, ICCV 2025 X-Sense Workshop.
- **Buckeye AutoDrive:** team captain and mobility innovation leader for an autonomous vehicle competition team at Ohio State.
